Why strength + protein

After 40, muscle is the thing worth protecting

We gradually lose muscle as we age, and it affects strength, balance and everyday energy. Research consistently points to the same two levers. Fit & Eat is built around both.

1

Resistance training, done regularly

Three short strength sessions a week, using your own body weight. Low impact, joint-friendly, and progressed gradually as you get stronger.

2

Enough protein, spread across the day

Every recipe shows its protein. Meals are planned so protein goes up on the days your muscles need it most, without supplements or shakes.

Joint-friendly by default

No jumping, no equipment. Swap anything that hurts.

Every exercise starts low impact and has an easier and a harder version. If something doesn't feel right, swap it in one tap and your plan stays balanced.

  • 3–4 short form cues for every move
  • The most common mistake, and how to avoid it
  • A clear “stop if you feel…” note
  • Simple, consistent line drawings for every exercise

Reverse lunge

Legs & glutes · 3 × 8 per side · Intermediate
  1. Stand tall, hands on hips or a chair for balance.
  2. Step one foot back and lower both knees gently.
  3. Keep your front knee over your ankle.
  4. Push through your front heel to stand up.
Common mistake: front knee drifting inward. Keep it in line with your toes.
Stop if you feel sharp pain in the knee or hip.
